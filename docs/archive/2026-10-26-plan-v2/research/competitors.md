### Claim 1 — Andela's first-90-day churn rate and soft-skill attribution

**Claim as used in the pitch:** "Andela's first-90-day churn is below 5%, and when matches fail,
it is usually soft skills, not technical ability."

**Source:** Investment Reports interview with Carrol Chang, Andela (published 23 June 2025).
URL: https://www.investmentreports.co/interview/carrol-chang-1839
**Date read:** YYYY-MM-DD _(fill in when you read it)_

**Exact quote:** "Our first 90-day churn rate is below 5%, which is industry-leading. When
matches do fail, it's usually due to soft skills—not technical ones. Issues like proactive
communication or cultural adaptation are common culprits."

**What it demonstrably supports:** Andela's own stated data: (a) 90-day churn is below 5%; (b)
failure attribution is soft skills, not technical.

**What it does not support:** That soft-skill risk is *predictable* from early follow-up signals. That
claim is a hypothesis, not in the source.

**Use in deck:** Slide 3 (problem). Cite: "Andela reports first-90-day churn below 5%, with
failures attributed to soft skills, not technical ability."

**Caveat:** This is an interview with an Andela executive, not an independent audit. The
number is Andela's own. The pitch should say "Andela reports" not "studies show."

### Claim 3 — Generation Kenya's outcome-based payout structure

**Claim as used in the pitch:** "Generation Kenya reduced assured payments to training
partners from 70% to 55%, with the remainder tied to placement and retention outcomes."

**Source:** Generation Kenya 2025-2029 Strategy (published July 2025). URL:
https://kenya.generation.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/20250619-Generation-Kenya-2025-20
29-Strategy-vApril-2025-2.pdf
**Date read:** YYYY-MM-DD _(fill in when you read it)_
**Exact quote:** "To drive greater accountability and results, we have introduced
outcome-based payouts, reducing assured payments to Training Partners from 70% to 55%,
with the remainder tied to placement and retention outcomes."

**What it demonstrably supports:** Funders (and funder-like intermediaries) are tying payment
to *placement and retention outcomes*, not just training delivery. This validates the problem:
retention is a measurable, financially material outcome.

**What it does not support:** That Generation Kenya or its partners would buy a tracking tool.
That funders would buy verification infrastructure (Pivot B). The structure exists; the buyer for
our product is not named.

**Use in deck:** Slide 3 (problem) or Slide 8 (pivot, secondary). Cite: "Generation Kenya now
ties 45% of partner payments to placement and retention outcomes."

**Caveat:** This is a strategy document, not a financial audit. The 70%→55% shift is as stated.
Do not extrapolate to other organisations without checking.

communication checks)

**Claim as used in the pitch (if needed):** "Andela's own onboarding model includes
30/60/90-day checkpoints and dedicated onboarding managers who monitor communication

**Source:** Andela, "White-Glove Support: Behind the Scenes Benefits of Staff Augmentation
2.0" (published 13 Dec 2020). URL:
https://wordpress.andela.com/insights/white-glove-support-behind-the-scenes-benefits-of-staff-a

**Secondary source:** Andela webinar page, "Best Practices for Onboarding Remote
Engineers" (published 8 April 2020). URL:
https://wordpress.andela.com/resources/on-demand-webinar/on-demand-webinar-best-practice
s-for-onboarding-remote-engineers/
**Date read:** YYYY-MM-DD _(fill in when you read it)_

**Exact quote (primary):** "Onboarding support ensures that engineers arrive at engagements
ready to contribute and are up to speed within 90 days. Onboarding managers monitor
developer performance during this period… Check-ins primarily cover: Engineer communication,
Professionalism."
**Exact quote (secondary):** "Set expectations for the first week and 30/60/90 days."
**What it demonstrably supports:** Andela already operates a structured onboarding and
check-in system that emphasises soft skills (communication, professionalism). This is *evidence
that the signals matter* and that a sophisticated operator already tracks them.

**What it does not support:** That Andela would buy Umwayi-RM. That Andela does not already
have this. In fact, the source suggests Andela *does* have this — which is a competitive risk to
acknowledge in Q&A.

**Use in deck:** Not on a slide. Use in Q&A if asked "doesn't Andela already do this?" Answer:
"Yes — Andela's onboarding model is sophisticated. Our thesis is not that Andela lacks process.
It is that the *signal* from that process is not packaged as a product for the *employer* who
bears the churn cost. Andela is a service provider; the employer is the buyer."

**Caveat:** This source is from 2020. Andela's model may have changed. Do not overstate its
current form.

### YYYY-MM-DD — Cross-product synthesis

- The *training-pipeline-specific* retention signal (not general workforce attrition). Hypothesis.

- The *deterministic queue as data collection* (not just a task list). Hypothesis.

**Verdict for the pitch:**
- Do not claim category novelty.

(employer who hires from pipelines).

- Has any retention analytics vendor targeted training-pipeline hires specifically?

instead.

confirmed the claim in this file.

is still accurate.
