# docs/research/interviews.md: what programme staff say

Purpose: real evidence for the problem. Nothing here is invented. If this file is empty on Nov 3, the video says the problem is a hypothesis.

Rules: ask permission before recording a quote. Remove names and organisations unless they agree. Store no learner data.

Questions (ten minutes):
1. How do you remember who needs a follow-up this week?
2. What was the last learner you lost touch with? What happened?
3. Where do notes live today (paper, spreadsheet, WhatsApp)?
4. Would you trust a tool to draft a message you approve? What would worry you?
5. Would you speak a note into your phone after a session?
