## Size and practice

- Press report (People Daily): the Ministry of Co-operatives is said to state that chamas manage over Sh300 billion. This is a news report of a ministry statement. The same piece suggests digital tool adoption is low. That is the columnist's observation and no source is given.
- Swisscontact (programme material): savings groups of about 10 to 30 members commonly keep paper records that are prone to errors.

## Existing tools

Chamasoft, M-Chama, Fundi Chama and a Postbank chama app were found. We read their public descriptions only, not their products. We do not claim to be the first record-keeping tool for groups. What we try differently: start from voice notes and paper pages groups already produce, ask before guessing, label evidence tiers. Whether that matters to treasurers is not known.

## Regulation

- Kenya Data Protection Act: the regulator's published guidance lists registration, consent, impact assessments for high-risk processing, retention limits and 72-hour breach notification. We did not assess whether a group tool needs registration. The demo uses synthetic data only. We make no compliance claim.
- Lending and deposit-taking can carry financial regulation. The product does neither.
