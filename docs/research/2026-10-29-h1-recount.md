## Reading

The pack's edge is visible in what finishes, not only in what is right. The generic prompt leaves 13 of 72 clear tasks unfinished (18%) against 4 of 72 (6%) for the curated pack, while both ask or stall rather than guess on ambiguous notes. This supports the reported story that the pack's value is precision in when to ask.
