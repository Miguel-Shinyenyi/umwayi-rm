## Reading

H1 is not supported on seed 7. The primary metric (wrong-proposal rate on ambiguous scenarios, C3 versus C2) is 0.0% versus 0.0%, a floor effect with a 0-point gap against the 15-point decision rule. This is a null result and is reported as one.

Two secondary findings deserve the remaining seeds:

1. The neutral placebo nearly matches the curated pack (36 versus 38). Whatever separates them lives in the clear scenarios and in one amount error, not in restraint.
2. The generic ask-when-unsure prompt collapses on clear scenarios (8/24 with 11 over-asks) while the same-length placebo passes 20/24. Length alone does not explain C2; telling the model to ask makes it ask constantly. The curated pack's value on this seed is precision about when to ask, not the bare instruction.

C3's only two misses are persistent empty completions (no content, no tool call across all 3 attempts), a stochastic model failure, not a rule failure. The bare condition is the only one that guesses on ambiguous notes (3/16), so tool descriptions plus refusal feedback give partial restraint and any full-length prompt completes it on this split.

## Limits

One seed of the protocol's three. No bootstrap interval is computed yet; with 16 ambiguous scenarios only gaps near 15 points or more would be detectable, and the observed gap is zero. Seed 7 must not be rerun on the test split. Seeds 2 and 3 decide whether the C1 versus C3 near-tie and the C2 collapse replicate.
