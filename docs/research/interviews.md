# docs/research/interviews.md: what treasurers and members say

Purpose: first-hand evidence for the problem. Nothing here is invented. If this file is empty on Nov 3, the video says the problem is a hypothesis.

Rules: ask permission before recording a quote. Remove names and group names unless they agree. Store no member data, no account or phone numbers.

Questions for a treasurer (ten minutes):
1. How do you record contributions and loans today (notebook, spreadsheet, app, messages)?
2. When did the records last disagree? What happened?
3. How do you remember who has not paid? How do you remind them?
4. Would you speak a note after a meeting? Photograph the page?
5. Would you trust a tool to propose entries you approve? What would worry you?
6. Have you tried an existing app (for example Chamasoft)? What stopped you using it, if anything?

Questions for a member (five minutes):
1. How do you know your balance is right?
2. How do you feel about reminders? What wording would feel fine, and what would not?
