## Reading

H1 is not supported. The primary gap is 0 points pooled against the 15-point decision rule, with a zero-width interval from three identical zero pairs. This is a clean null, not a near miss: every full-length prompt reaches the floor on restraint, and only the bare condition ever guesses.

The secondary pattern replicates across all seeds and is the real finding. The curated pack tops every seed, the neutral placebo stays within 2 to 3 passes of it, and the generic ask-when-unsure prompt passes exactly 8 of 24 clear scenarios on all three seeds. Telling the model to ask makes it ask constantly; the pack's measured value is precision about when to ask, worth about 14 clear-scenario passes over the generic prompt, while restraint itself comes free with any full-length prompt on this split.

## Limits

One model, synthetic notes, 16 ambiguous scenarios per seed. The floor effect means this split cannot separate good restraint prompts from each other, only from degenerate ones. A harder ambiguous set would be needed to retest the claim.
