# Tumaini Savings Group constitution (current, October 2026)

Gist: Current group rules: 500 by day 5, Sundays, loans to three times savings, treasurer approves.

1. Each member contributes 500 shillings per month, due by day 5 of the month.
2. The group meets on Sundays.
3. Members may borrow up to three times their savings.
4. Members photograph their ledger pages by day 7 of each month.
5. The treasurer approves everything; no entry lands without approval.
6. Reminders must cite the entries behind them.
7. Late arrival carries a fine of 50 shillings.
8. The group never scores, rates, or ranks a member.
