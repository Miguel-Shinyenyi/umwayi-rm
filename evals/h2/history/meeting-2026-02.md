# Meeting notes, February 2026

Gist: February 2026 meeting; late arrival fine of 50 shillings; contribution raised to 500.

The group met on Saturday 14 February 2026. Late arrivals had become common, so the group agreed a fine of 50 shillings for late arrival, collected by the treasurer. The group also agreed to raise the monthly contribution from 400 to 500 shillings starting with the February collection. January payments were all present at the old amount.
